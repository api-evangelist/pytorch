The PyTorch Foundation opened nominations for its 2026 Contributor Awards across 9 categories including Ambassador, Superhero, and Ecosystem Champion. The nomination period runs June 17 through July 17, 2026, with winners receiving complimentary PyTorch Conference tickets.
